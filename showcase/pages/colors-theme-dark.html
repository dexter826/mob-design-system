Dark theme palette

Every color value the system defines. The dark scale is the only theme in the source: a matte charcoal neutral ramp, two border steps, three text steps and four semantic states. The accent is the one value the source does not provide — it is corrected to neon green #00cc5c, held at the same luminance as info and warning, and the archive volt #10e569 it replaced is kept as a recorded provenance value.

  • Neutral rampFive charcoal steps from #0a0a0a to #222222. The canvas is the darkest value, cards sit one step above it at #121212 and never lighter. Panel headers, zebra rows and item hover use #181818; popovers, dropdowns and modals use #202020.
  • Borders and hoverEvery 1px rule is #262626, stepping to #383838 on hover and on an active card. Hover moves one surface level up (#0a0a0a to #181818, #181818 to #222222) and one border level up; it never lightens text.
  • Semantic statesSuccess #00cc5c — an alias of the accent — warning #f59e0b, error #ef4444, info #38bdf8. Operational and action share one green deliberately: two greens 2.8 degrees apart measure 1.24:1 against each other, under the 3:1 non-text floor, which reads as a rendering fault rather than two meanings, so the word, the 6px square and the icon carry the difference instead. Status words stay in sentence case: operational, degraded, failed, pending. Info blue carries system messages and links only and must not compete with the accent for a primary action.
  • Single themeThese are all the values the system publishes, so nothing here has a paired token to switch to. Most are measured. The accent family — #00cc5c, #00e065, #00b853 and rgba(0, 204, 92,.1) — is corrected, and rgba(0, 204, 92,.1) is authored: the archive's own accent tint, re-scoped to the success role. No light palette is published; the light theme card records that absence.
