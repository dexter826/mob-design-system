Accessibility

Requirements, working examples and the limits of automated evidence.

Text contrast

Normal text needs at least 4.5:1 for WCAG 2.2 AA. Large text needs 3:1, at a minimum of 24 CSS px regular or 18.667 CSS px bold (700+). A 14px label does not qualify as large text.

Use secondary text for timestamps, placeholders and keyboard labels. Use foreground error copy with a red marker on elevated surfaces. Muted is restricted to disabled/decorative content or qualifying large text with at least 3:1 contrast.

Project names must contain at least one non-space character.

Controls and focus

Essential control boundaries use control-border. Low-contrast separators and a 1.06:1 canvas/surface fill difference do not identify a control on their own. Every focusable control retains the same visible focus ring.

Press Tab to inspect keyboard focus.

Fine-pointer targets are at least 24×24 CSS px. On coarse pointers, shared controls grow to 44px; the hit areas do not overlap. Target-size rules include exceptions and do not certify a whole interface.

Keyboard-operated tabs

Arrow keys, Home and End select enabled tabs. Tab moves into the active panel. Each tab is linked to its panel; disabled tabs are skipped.

Configuration updated. Long content wraps without clipping its meaning.

Verification scope

Token arithmetic and regression checks provide evidence for specific cases. They do not replace screen-reader, zoom and browser testing. See the execution report for checks actually run and pending work.
