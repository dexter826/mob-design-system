All five supplied PNG exports in png/ measure 1200 x 1200, including the horizontal lockups and social cover. The SVG social cover is correctly sized at 1200 x 630. Use SVG or the full-frame PNG exports in logos/exports/. A print master, proof sheets and redistribution licence were not included.

Icons

authored for this system

The archive supplied no icon and no icon font, so the family in assets/icons/icons.svg was drawn for this package: a 24×24 grid, 1.5px stroke, butt caps, miter joins, orthogonal geometry plus 45° diagonals only, and currentColor throughout. It is labelled authored brand work — part of the system, but never citable as a source measurement. The present chip is filled with the success tint rgba(0, 204, 92,.1) and the accent word #00cc5c: an availability or health marker takes the same green as the accent, and the square plus the word is what says so. Gallery and pairing rules: preview/icons.html.

All three faces are Google Fonts builds of upstream projects under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and the licence text travels with the bundle: spacegrotesk.OFL.txt, plusjakartasans.OFL.txt, jetbrainsmono.OFL.txt. OFL requires the notice to ship beside the font files, which is why they are stored rather than linked. Manifest: fonts/licenses.json.
