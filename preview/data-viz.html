Data visualisation

Charts

Four series tokens, one reserved accent, 1px gridlines and mono tabular figures. The selected series is green; ordinary series are blue and neutral, so the brand's accent rule survives inside a plot. Everything below is inline SVG painted from colors_and_type.css — no canvas, no chart library, no CDN.

Multi-series line chart with a threshold

p95 latency by region, last 24 hoursy: p95 latency (ms) · x: time (UTC, 3 h buckets)
ap-southeast-1 — selected eu-west-1 us-east-1 SLO 250 ms

Three series is the documented shape: two ordinary ones on --chart-series-1 and --chart-series-2, one selected on --chart-series-selected. Markers are 4px squares on the selected line only, so a hue is never the only cue — every line ends in its own name, and the legend repeats it. The flat fill under us-east-1 uses --chart-area-fill, which is registered in the series-1 hue, so it may only sit under that series. The dashed objective is error-coloured and labelled "SLO 250 ms": the hue carries meaning only because the word is there, and the sentence would still read with the line drawn in grey.

Bar chart

Deploys per day, this weeky: deploys (count) · x: weekday

Bars are square — radius 0px, no rounded cap, no gradient. The gap between bars is 24px and the floor is 4px: below that a bar stops being separable from its neighbour at 100% zoom. Counts sit above each bar right-aligned to its edge in JetBrains Mono with tabular-nums; the axis states the unit once, so the ticks are bare digits. Friday is the selected day: the bar takes --chart-series-selected and the word "Selected" is printed above it, because a green bar on its own would only read as "success".

A sparkline is 24px tall (--sparkline-height), has no axis, no gridline, no tick and no label of its own — the stat cell already names the metric and prints the current figure. It ends in one 4px square marker at the latest point. The delta is a signed number plus an arrow glyph plus a word; never a colour on its own. The first cell draws an ordinary metric in --chart-series-1, the second is the selected metric and takes the accent, so each cell holds at most one accent element: here the line carries the selection, which is why its delta text stays neutral rather than also turning green.

Rules

Chart contract
RuleValueWhy
Series cap4--chart-series-1…4 is the whole ramp. A fifth series aggregates into "other" or facets into small multiples; it does not add a hue, because the palette has no fifth registered colour and an invented one breaks dark-only consistency.
What the accent may mean2 usesInside a chart the accent marks the selected series or a threshold crossing — never both at once, never a default line colour, never a background wash. Ordinary series take #38bdf8 plus the neutral ramp.
Threshold1 labelDashed --chart-dash-2 (8px 4px) in --color-error, labelled with the target and the number ("SLO 250 ms"). A dashed line without a word is decoration.
Ticks≤ 4--chart-y-ticks is 4. No minor gridlines, no second axis on the same face — facet instead. The state below is the whole set: 0, 100, 200, 300.
FiguresmonoJetBrains Mono with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums, right-aligned in a column or on an axis, so digits do not jitter when a value updates. Units live in the axis label, not on every tick.
Marker geometry4px sq--chart-marker is a 4px square; --chart-line is 2px. No circles, no rounded caps, no mitered glow — the same square architecture the rest of the system uses.
FillflatOne flat --chart-area-fill under one series. No gradient, no 3D, no drop shadow under a line, no glow, no blur, no second fill.
LabellingdirectEnd-of-line labels beat a legend, because the reader never has to carry a hue across the plot. A legend is kept only where the swatch pairs with the region name; a swatch alone is not a label.
Line contrast≥ 3:1All four series tokens clear 3:1 against the card surface #121212. The weakest is --chart-series-3 (#737373) at 3.95:1 — above the data-line floor, below the 4.5:1 text floor, so it never carries an axis label. Axis text uses --chart-label (#a1a1a1).
Structural rulesdecorativeGridlines are 1px #181818 on #121212, and a 1px #262626 border is 1.31:1 on canvas: neither reaches the 3:1 floor. They are scaffolding and must never be the sole carrier of meaning; the baseline uses --chart-axis (#383838) instead.
Reduced motion0.01msA chart never animates in this system — no draw-on, no staggered bars, no dash-march, no value count-up. So prefers-reduced-motion changes nothing here; the chart is already the reduced-motion state. Hover only steps the card border to --color-border-strong at 0.12s.

Alias coverage: the contract publishes public aliases for --chart-series-1…4 and --chart-series-selected only. Grid, axis, label, area fill, line width, marker size, dash patterns, sparkline height and the tick count exist solely under the --mob-signal-* prefix, so this card resolves them by their full names — --mob-signal-chart-grid, --mob-signal-chart-axis, --mob-signal-chart-label, --mob-signal-chart-area-fill, --mob-signal-chart-line, --mob-signal-chart-marker, --mob-signal-chart-dash-2, --mob-signal-sparkline-height — and the short names used in the table above are the alias forms to register in the contract.

Provenance: historical design input (not bundled) ships no chart, no sparkline, no series ramp and no axis colour — only component CSS. Every value on this card is therefore authored from the registered palette in design-tokens.json plus the 2px/1px border arithmetic, not measured from a rendered reference. The two measured anchors it inherits are the 24px/32px type steps and the 4px spacing grid.
