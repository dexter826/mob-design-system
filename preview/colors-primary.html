Color

Primary colors

Neon green #00cc5c is the only brand accent. It marks the primary action, the selection, the keyboard focus ring and the selected chart series, and nothing else. The Operational marker runs on the same green: an action and a state are told apart by the word, the 6px square and the icon, never by a second shade of the same hue. Text on the accent fill is #000000; black is the only label color the accent takes, because #ededed on it measures 1.83:1.

  • Where the accent is allowedPrimary CTA fill, selected and active indicators, the focus ring, the selected chart series and the Operational marker. No other use is documented: no background wash, no glow, no colored shadow. It emits the same relative luminance as the info blue and the warning amber — 0.4396 against 0.4401 and 0.4389 — which is what stops a filled button from out-shining the panel it sits in. At full saturation the neon still halates, so it never carries text below 14px: "neon" names saturation, not light.
  • ProvenanceThe accent family is a corrected value, not an archive measurement; the archive volt #10e569 is retained as a recorded value only. At 100% sRGB saturation, hue 147 couples chroma to luminance, so C 0.262 can only be emitted at luminance 0.7229 — 14.57:1 on the canvas, which out-shines every panel around it. #00cc5c holds the hue and the full saturation and sits at luminance 0.4396, where C 0.206 is the sRGB ceiling: the most chromatic green available at that brightness, not a compromise, and level with info at 0.4401 and warning at 0.4389. Hover #00e065 and active #00b853 are steps computed against that base and clear 1.21:1 and 1.23:1 from it. A hover step around 1.01:1 against its own base is no visible state change on a 32px fill, so every state stop is measured against the base it ships with.
  • ContrastMeasured WCAG 2.2: #000000 on #00cc5c is 9.79:1, on the hover #00e065 11.85:1 and on the active #00b853 7.98:1 — all AAA. #ededed on the accent is 1.83:1, a fail, which is why black is the only label color on an accent fill. Foreground #ededed on canvas is 16.91:1, secondary #a1a1a1 7.66:1, and muted #737373 4.18:1 — below the 4.5:1 AA line, so muted stays placeholders, disabled text and 18px metadata.
  • A tint never selectsAccent #00cc5c reads 9.23:1 on canvas, 8.73:1 on surface, 8.28:1 on surface-subtle and 7.60:1 on surface-elevated. But the 10% accent fill measures 1.16:1 against surface, far under the 3:1 non-text floor. So a selected row, tab or option always adds the 2px accent edge; the tint is decoration.
