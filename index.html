Open Design system

M.O.B Signal

Sharp, square, dark-mode product UI with one disciplined neon accent. Neutral, type and geometry are measured from design-tokens.json and design-system.css in the source archive; the accent is the one corrected value, and success is an alias of it.

  • Color paletteSeven engine roles: background #0a0a0a, surface #121212, foreground #ededed, muted #737373, border #262626, accent #00cc5c, accent-secondary #38bdf8 — plus success, an alias of the accent with no engine role of its own. Ratios are measured, not quoted: foreground 16.91:1 on canvas, black on the accent 9.79:1, muted 4.18:1 and therefore not a small-text color.
  • TypographySpace Grotesk for display at weights 500, 600 and 700; Plus Jakarta Sans for body at 400, 500, 600 and 700; JetBrains Mono for code and hex values at 400, 500 and 600. Seven steps, from 12px to 32px.
  • Voice and toneSentence case everywhere, including buttons, labels, table headers and nav. Actions are verb-first: "Deploy new service", "Save changes", "View audit log". No all-caps labels, no noun-only buttons, no superlatives without a measurement.
  • ImageryNo photography and no illustration. Structure comes from typography, 1px rules and status color. Product captures keep the dark canvas and square framing, cropped to the UI, with no device mockup, drop shadow or colored overlay.
  • LayoutRadius is 0px on every element. Borders are 1px and step to #383838 on hover and active. Spacing comes from the 4px scale only: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48. Motion is a single 0.12s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) on background-color, border-color and color.
  • ComponentsMeasured anatomy: Button (primary, secondary, ghost, danger, disabled), Card, Form (label, input, hint, focus, error), Navigation, Data Table, Badge, Kbd and the keyboard focus ring. Control height is 32px. Elevation is black-only; the focus ring is 0 0 0 2px #0a0a0a, 0 0 0 4px #00cc5c. Authored on top of it: checkbox, radio, switch, slider, select, date picker, file upload, tabs, segmented control, accordion, progress, modal, drawer, menu, popover, tooltip, toast, command palette, pagination, breadcrumbs, avatar, skeleton and empty state, each with five states.
  • IconsA 24-grid family authored for this system: 1.5px stroke, butt caps, miter joins, square geometry, currentColor, three sizes. No icon font, no emoji, no accent-colored glyph outside the selected and healthy states, and no icon alone carries a status.
  • Motion0.12s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) on background-color, border-color and color. Overlays fade on opacity only at 0.12s in and 0.08s out, determinate progress moves width at 0.24s, stagger is 0s, and prefers-reduced-motion collapses all of it. No spinner, no shimmer, no slide, no scale.
  • DataFour series on secondary blue plus the neutral ramp, the accent reserved for the selected series or a threshold crossing. 2px lines, 4px square markers, square bars, four ticks, direct end labels, tabular mono figures.
  • Layout12 columns on a 24px gap, breakpoints at 640, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1536, a 1200px app container and a 920px prose measure, a 48px navigation band that reserves its own height, a 240px sidebar, a 360px drawer, modals at 480, 640 and 880, and a single z-index ladder from 1 to 130.
  • AccessibilityRatios are measured, not quoted. #737373 at 4.18:1 is demoted below body-text duty; the 1px rule at 1.31:1 never identifies a control alone; targets are 32px visual, 24px minimum, 44px on touch. Keyboard focus is visible on everything.
